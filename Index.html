About Us

Since 2015, The Nose has offered one of the most carefully curated selections of 100% authentic brand name fragrances online. We specialize in both contemporary and rare/vintage perfumes, carrying over 800 premium brands that we ship across the Philippines, Asia, and worldwide! Our mission is to provide every customer a personalized, educational, and memorable experience offering not only original luxury brand name products but also exceptional customer service. Whether you're discovering your first signature scent or hunting for that elusive vintage bottle, our team of passionate fragrance specialists is here to guide your journey with expert recommendations and our authenticity guarantee.
